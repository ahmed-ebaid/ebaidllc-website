Applied AI Consulting for Business

AI is useful when it solves a specific problem, not simply because a model can produce an impressive demo. Ebaid LLC helps businesses choose practical use cases, understand the data and integration work involved, and define what a successful result should look like.

Based in Naperville, Illinois, Ebaid LLC is led by Ahmed Ebaid, PhD, whose experience includes enterprise cloud systems and involvement in AI projects at Microsoft.

Start with the workflow, not the model

A useful first engagement examines one existing process: who performs it, what information they need, where delays occur, and which mistakes would be costly. Candidate uses might include summarizing documents, assisting engineers, or organizing information for a human decision.

Some steps are better handled by a search query, a rule, or a conventional application. Separating those steps from work that needs a language model keeps the proposed solution easier to evaluate and maintain.

What an assessment can deliver

  • A scoped use case: intended users, inputs, outputs, exclusions, and the human decision the system supports.
  • A data-access plan: required sources, permissions, sensitive information, and boundaries on what can be sent to a model.
  • A prototype plan: the smallest useful workflow, integration dependencies, and a way to compare it with the current process.
  • Evaluation criteria: representative examples, acceptable error rates for the task, response-time and cost considerations, and cases that require escalation.

The scope and deliverables are agreed before implementation. An assessment can also conclude that AI is not the right approach for the selected problem.

From prototype to a usable application

A prototype should be tested on representative inputs, including incomplete information and requests outside its intended scope. Before wider use, the design needs a clear owner, a feedback path, and a plan for handling unsupported or incorrect responses.

When the use case requires tools or actions, our AI agent integration service focuses on permissions, review gates, and repeatable workflows. For production visibility and recovery planning, see cloud reliability and observability consulting.

How should you prepare for an AI consultation?

Describe one process you want to improve, the people who use it, and how you measure the current result. Bring a few non-sensitive examples of inputs and desired outputs. You do not need to choose a model or grant access to confidential systems to start the discussion.
