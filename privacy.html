Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Introduction

Ebaid LLC ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile applications and services collect, use, and safeguard your information.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • App preferences and settings (e.g., selected reciter, bookmarks, reading position)
  • Contact information if you reach out to us for support

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App usage analytics (crash reports, performance data)
  • We do NOT collect location data, contacts, photos, or other sensitive device data

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain app functionality
  • To save your preferences and reading progress locally on your device
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs
  • To respond to your support requests

Data Storage

Your reading preferences, bookmarks, and app settings are stored locally on your device. We do not maintain user accounts or store personal data on external servers. Audio and text content may be cached on your device for offline use.

Third-Party Services

Our apps may use the following third-party services:

  • Quran APIs — to retrieve Quran text, translations, and tafseer content
  • Audio CDNs — to stream recitation audio
  • Firebase Crashlytics (if enabled) — for anonymous crash reporting

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The apps are educational in nature and designed to be safe for all ages.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share anonymous, aggregated analytics data to improve our services.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete all app data by uninstalling the application
  • Clear local storage and caches from your device settings
  • Contact us to request information about any data we may hold

Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of our apps after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
