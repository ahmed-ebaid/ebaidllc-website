About the Founder

Ahmed Ebaid

PhD in Computer Science · Senior Software Engineer at Microsoft

Ebaid LLC was founded by Ahmed Ebaid, a software engineer with over 10 years of industry experience spanning cloud infrastructure, mobile development, and large-scale system design.

Ahmed's experience at Microsoft includes Azure cloud infrastructure, including Azure VMware Solution, and involvement in AI projects spanning engineering workflows and operational automation. At Ebaid LLC, that background informs a practical approach to AI integration, cloud architecture, and software delivery — with evaluation and human review for consequential actions.

Prior to Microsoft, Ahmed held senior engineering roles at Allstate and Hitachi Data Systems, delivering enterprise solutions across iOS, backend systems, and security-critical platforms for clients including Wells Fargo and KPN.
