About the Founder

Dr. Ahmed Ebaid

PhD in Computer Science · Senior Software Engineer at Microsoft

Ebaid LLC was founded by Dr. Ahmed Ebaid, a software engineer with over 10 years of industry experience spanning cloud infrastructure, mobile development, and large-scale system design.

Ahmed currently serves as a Senior Software Engineer and Team Lead at Microsoft, where he leads innovation in Azure cloud infrastructure — improving performance, reliability, and customer experience at scale.

Prior to Microsoft, Ahmed held senior engineering roles at Allstate and Hitachi Data Systems, delivering enterprise solutions across iOS, backend systems, and security-critical platforms for clients including Wells Fargo and KPN.
